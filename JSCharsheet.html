Default (maximum) values are determined automatically based on your Affinities, but Points will go down as you play. Hit Points represent how much damage your character can take. Mastery Points represent your character's ability to supplement luck with skill for better outcomes. Spirit Points represent your character's inner strength, which can be expended to produce magical spells or other supernatural effects.

Affinities

Affinities are styles or preferences for how your character approaches challenges. Physicality represents reliance on the body, often in a very simple or direct way. Technique represents reliance on practice, precision, and process. It encompasses both careful physical movements and precise, informed mental movements. Spirit represents reliance on strength of soul, willpower, even luck. It is the energy by which magical, extraordinary, or supernatural effects are brought to reality.

Roll Summary

Affinity: Result of your Affinity die roll. Strength of the Affinity determines number of sides on the die (4, 8, 12). Proficiency: Result of your Proficiency die roll. Any relevant modifiers to the Proficiency die are automatically applied before being displayed here. Mastery: Tap here to spend Mastery Points to improve your Total. MP are automatically deducted when tapped in this way. Total: the sum of your Affinity + Proficiency + Mastery. Degree: represents how much of an effect your action has. Degree increases with each full increment of 5 of your Total.

Equipment
Starting Equipment - All characters start play with a unique Signature Item, the precise nature of which is determined by the player, and a Bag of Convenience.
Signature Item: an item unique to you that gives you +1 to a single Proficiency of your choice. Examples might include an enchanted sword that gives +1 to Offense, or a family heirloom toolkit that gives +1 to Artifice, or perhaps a battered but reliable shield that gives +1 to Defense.

Bag of Convenience: A small sack that magically produces utterly ordinary, non-electronic items that could be purchased at a real-life dollar store for not more than five real-life dollars. Items produced by the Bag last for about 10 minutes or until they break or another item is produced. Items produced by the Bag seem to respond negatively to excessive ambition and may spontaneously vanish if too much is demanded of them.
